Apologies

Jan 25, 2026 in OPEN SOURCE • TECHNOLOGY
3 min read

I owe a few folks some apologies from my brief period as Executive Director. Here goes.

First off, I sincerely apologize to Jehan Pagès and Christian Hergert. I spoke rudely to both of you and neither of you deserved it. Months of escalating psychological abuse reached their apex at GUADEC this past July. I am not prone to anxiety. But from July 23rd to August 29th, I would only fall asleep from utter physical exhaustion and would wake up two or three hours later with a panic attack. I have never experienced sleep deprivation like this and, in conjunction with the abuse, it really drove me crazy. But that was no excuse for the way I spoke to either of you. I’m sorry.

Next, I apologize to Holly, Julian, Deb, other Julian, Adrian, Lorenz, and so many other helpful community members. Holly, you warned me. Twice. Folks on the hiring team dropped hints during interviews pointing to the real problems at the Foundation. The rest of you were more direct… but you sounded like conspiracy theorists. Going back over my notebooks from 2025, everything makes a lot more sense now. But it’s too late.

To the many, many people who complained to me about the behaviour of the GNOME Code of Conduct Committee: I’m sorry. You were trying to point to the fact that the CoCC is, obviously, comprised of people. I was too preoccupied with getting the Foundation’s head above water, financially. So allow me to be direct where you were not: the Code of Conduct Committee is broken and the board must address it.

If that statement causes the CoCC to remove this post in self-defense, you can find a copy here: gnome-apologies.md

Sonny

To Sonny Piers: I am sorry. On June 6th, I had a call with you that lasted nearly four hours. Without naming people or making accusations, you told me your complicated story. You seemed hurt. But based on what a couple people in positions of authority told me about you, I thought you were faking it. I took five pages of notes… but I took them back to the wrong person.

Your situation and my situation were very different. But the cast of characters was the same and so was the outcome. I know now you weren’t faking it. You didn’t deserve what the CoCC did to you.

Tobias

More than anyone, I want to apologize to Tobias Bernard. I am sorry. I had multiple 3-hour conversations with you. You were always patient with me. You shared all your ideas with me openly. I have countless pages of notes. You were willing to give your time selflessly to the improvement of GNOME and to repairing the Foundation.

I listened to everything you had to say… until you brought up the topic of Sonny. Once again, someone with a lot of authority had convinced me that you had been manipulated. I argued with you. I didn’t listen. You deserved better from me.

Sonny is lucky to have a friend like you.
